THE EXPLORER’S FIELD GUIDE

Field protocol.

You and the computer ranger alternate turns. Each turn rolls a four-sided die, moves along the trail and may reveal an animal or trigger a terrain feature.

Animal encounter chance is 50%; each of the five species is equally likely. Terrain effects have a separate 50% chance if you land on a feature. Only the landed tile is checked; effects do not chain.

The first explorer to reach the final tile earns 10 bonus points and ends the game immediately. The player with the highest total score wins, even if the other player reached the finish first. Equal scores are a draw.

Negative scores are allowed. Movement stays within the trail. A shared seed reproduces the trail, dice and encounters; this is a chance-based game, not a tracking or navigation service.

Starting a new expedition replaces the current game on this page. Browser sessions are kept in server memory and expire when the server restarts or after 100 newer games. An exceptional 1,000-turn safety limit ends the game by score without a finish bonus.
